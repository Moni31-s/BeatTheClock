# BeatTheClock
Beat the Clock helps you build speed for timed exams. Many students know the material but freeze when the timer runs. This app trains that skill step by step: it starts at 30 seconds per question and slowly drops to 15, so the pressure grows gradually instead of all at once. 

A friendly buddy (bear, cat, bunny or panda) cheers you on and never makes you feel bad for a wrong answer.
Make your own questions in seconds, with no complicated format.
See your average answer time and review the questions you missed.
Pick your own colors, light or dark mode, and turn sound on or off.

Practice a little each day, and 15 seconds will start to feel slow.
